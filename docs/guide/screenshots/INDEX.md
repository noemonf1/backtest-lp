# Screenshot index

All images were taken with headless Chromium (Playwright 1.62) at a 1600 px wide viewport on 2026-09-06. "Live" means the Railway deployment at https://backtest-lp-production.up.railway.app/. "Local" means the app served from this checkout at http://127.0.0.1:8501 with the August 2026 files present in `data/`. Full-page images were produced by resizing the viewport to the page height (Streamlit scrolls inside its main container, so Playwright's own full-page mode captures one viewport only). Tight images are crops of a full-page capture or element screenshots. The capture scripts and the step log are in `docs/guide/raw/shots_*`.
